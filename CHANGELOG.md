# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- 15 levels in 15 worlds, plus an endless mode
- Four chasers: the Thief, Kernel Panic, Segfault Monster and Proprietary Lock-in
- Standing and oncoming trains, ramps, firewalls, cables, zombie processes and ice
- Fish, golden fish and open-source keys
- Power-ups: magnet, Tux-board, jetpack, super jump and coffee (x2)
- Missions with a permanent score multiplier
- Five skins
- 26 languages with automatic detection
- Remappable keyboard and gamepad controls, plus touch and mouse swipes
- Procedurally generated graphics, sound effects and music
